
#  CampusMart

**First HTML Hackathon Project**
📅 November 2024 — *HACK Oasis Hackathon*

---

## 🚀 Overview

CampusMart is a student-focused marketplace designed specifically for the Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering community. The platform allows students to **buy, sell, and rent items within campus**, making everyday student needs more affordable, accessible, and convenient.

Instead of relying on external platforms, CampusMart creates a **trusted, college-specific ecosystem** where students can directly interact and exchange items.

---

## 💡 Problem Statement

College students frequently face issues such as:

* High costs of new books, electronics, and essentials
* Difficulty finding short-term rentals (e.g., lab equipment, calculators)
* Lack of a centralized platform for student-to-student transactions

CampusMart solves this by providing a **simple and interactive web platform** tailored to campus needs.
